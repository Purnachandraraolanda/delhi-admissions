# Edu9 Delhi Universities Admission Guidance - PRD

## Original Problem Statement
Build a webpage as a subdomain of edu9.in regarding Delhi universities with:
- 4 university sections: DTU, DU/CPU, IPU, Top Private Universities in Delhi
- Related YouTube videos (7 total) in grid layout without video names
- Contact details, Google Maps embed, services list
- Slot booking link: https://bookings.edu9.in/#/2026
- Company logo integration
- Social media links: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp

## User Personas
1. **Students** - Seeking admission guidance for Delhi universities
2. **Parents** - Looking for trusted career counselling services
3. **College Aspirants** - Exploring options across DTU, DU, IPU, and private universities

## Core Requirements (Static)
- Professional navy blue & gold theme matching Edu9 branding
- Responsive single-page landing website
- Company logo displayed throughout
- Social media integration (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp)
- Floating WhatsApp button for instant queries
